# Хэвлэлийн тэмдэг, хэлбэр (typography and formatting)

## Y1. Em and en dashes

**Rule:** the final text has no em dashes (—) or en dashes (–) used as pauses, and no " -- ". AI uses them far more than Mongolian writers do. Replace with a comma, a period, a colon, or parentheses.
**Keep:** dashes in number ranges (2020–2024) and in dialogue if the author's text already uses them that way. A user writing sample that uses dashes overrides this rule.

> **Before:** Шинэ хууль — олон жилийн хэлэлцүүлгийн дараа — батлагдлаа.
> **After:** Олон жил хэлэлцсэний эцэст шинэ хууль батлагдлаа.

## Y2. Mixed quote marks

**Rule:** Mongolian uses both «...» and "...". Pick the one the author uses and keep it consistent. Never mix «» and "" and “” in the same text. If there is no sign of preference, use straight "...".

## Y3. Title Case headings in Cyrillic

**Watch for:** "Боловсролын Шинэчлэлийн Үр Дүн", "Бидний Үйлчилгээ".
**Why:** an English habit. Mongolian capitalizes only the first word and proper nouns.
**Fix:** "Боловсролын шинэчлэлийн үр дүн".

## Y4. Bold-label bullet lists

**Watch for:** lists where each item starts with a bold word and a colon: "**Давуу тал:** ...", "**Сорилт:** ...". Common in AI essays and posts.
**Fix:** in essays and letters, turn them into prose. In real documentation, plain lists are fine; drop the bold labels that just restate the item.

## Y5. Emoji and decorative symbols

**Watch for:** 🚀 ✅ 💡 📌 at the start of headings or bullets.
**Fix:** delete, unless the author's own casual post uses them.

## Y6. Headings in short texts

**Watch for:** an essay or a Facebook post split into "Оршил", "Үндсэн хэсэг", "Дүгнэлт" headings when nobody asked for them.
**Fix:** remove the headings and keep paragraphs. (Keep them if the assignment requires that structure.)
