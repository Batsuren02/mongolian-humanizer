# Хэвлэлийн тэмдэг, хэлбэр (typography and formatting)

All rules here are **[дүгнэлт]**: general AI formatting habits, not measured for Mongolian. Treat them as light signals and never let them override the author's own habits.

## Y1. Dash pauses

**Rule:** in expository prose (essays, letters, reports, posts), replace em or en dashes (—, –) used as mid-sentence pauses, and " -- ", with a comma, a colon, parentheses, or nothing.

**Keep:**
- Dialogue dashes at the start of a speech line ("— Сайн уу? гэв."), standard in Mongolian fiction.
- A dash between subject and predicate nouns ("Улаанбаатар — Монгол Улсын нийслэл").
- Number ranges (2020–2024).
- Any dash use in a user writing sample.

> **Before:** Шинэ хууль — олон жилийн хэлэлцүүлгийн дараа — батлагдлаа.
> **After:** Шинэ хууль олон жилийн хэлэлцүүлгийн дараа батлагдлаа.

## Y2. Mixed quote marks

Mongolian writers use «...» or "...". Keep whichever the author uses, consistently. Do not mix «», "", and “” in one text. With no sign of preference, use "...".

## Y3. Title Case headings in Cyrillic

**Watch for:** "Боловсролын Шинэчлэлийн Үр Дүн", "Бидний Шинэ Үйлчилгээ".
**Fix:** capitalize the first word and proper names only: "Боловсролын шинэчлэлийн үр дүн". Official names keep their capitals ("Монгол Улсын Их Хурал").

## Y4. Bold-label bullet lists

**Watch for:** lists where each item starts with a bold word and a colon ("**Давуу тал:** ...") inside essays and letters.
**Fix:** in essays and letters, turn them into prose. In documentation and instructions, plain lists are fine.

## Y5. Emoji

**Keep** emoji in posts, ads, and chats where they fit the genre or the author uses them.
**Remove** decorative emoji from essays, letters, reports, and headings of formal documents.

## Y6. Headings in short texts

**Watch for:** a one-page essay or a social post with explicit "Оршил", "Үндсэн хэсэг", "Дүгнэлт" headings nobody asked for. The three-part structure itself is taught in schools and is fine as paragraphs.
**Fix:** remove the headings and keep the paragraphs. Keep them if the assignment requires headings.
