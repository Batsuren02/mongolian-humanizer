# Найруулгын төрөл, өнгө аяс (register and tone)

## Five functional styles [эх сурвалж: Сүхбаатар; nairuulga.mn]

### Албан бичгийн найруулга (official)

- Fixed patterns (тогтсон хэв загвар), words in their literal sense, no imagery, no emotion, no colloquial or dialect words.
- Tone: requesting, directing, or deciding ("захирсан, хүссэн, тушаасан өнгө аяс").
- Structure: first the purpose and grounds, then the proposal, request, or decision.
- Formulas belong here: "Иймд ...-ыг хүсье", "...-ыг танилцуулж байна", "-тэй холбогдон", "үндсэн дээр", "Хүндэтгэсэн". Petitions address the recipient with "Танаа".
- Repetition of a term is allowed (precision beats variety).
- Email: be brief, skip ceremonial greetings ("ёсорхуу мэндчилгээ").

**What to fix here:** noun + хийх/хийгдэх, agentless passives, -лт chains that hide who does what. **What not to touch:** the formulas, "Та", the formal rung, balanced pairs like "өргөжүүлэн бэхжүүлэх".

### Шинжлэх ухааны найруулга (academic)

- Logic, one-meaning terms, no ornament, literal words, "оновчтой ... цөөн үгээр, онож өгүүлэх".
- Long compound sentences and some nominalisation are normal ("үйлийг нэршүүлэн нэрлэх нь их").

**Fix only:** calques, redundant plurals, chatbot leftovers, invented sources. Leave long sentences and terms.

### Сонин нийтлэлийн найруулга (newspaper, public writing)

- Plain and comprehensible; stock phrases are normal (Сүхбаатар); a little moving, vivid language used fittingly makes it stronger.
- News sentences over about 25 words are risky (journalism textbook advice, news only).

### Уран зохиолын найруулга (literary)

- Synonyms and antonyms, alliteration (толгой, сүүл холбох), even rhythm, proverbs, imagery.
- Avoid repeating a word; vary with synonyms.
- Do not apply this style's ornaments to plain essays or reports.

### Ярианы найруулга (conversational)

- Short turns, particles (шүү, даа, л, биз), -лаа/-сан endings, spoken word order.
- In posts and chats, keep the author's slang, Latin-script Mongolian, and emoji habits.

## School essays (эсээ, зохион бичлэг) [эх сурвалж: ЕБС-ийн үнэлгээний шалгуур 2021]

- Grades 10 to 12 write explanatory, reflective, and persuasive essays. The 12th-grade criterion: persuade using evidence, imagery (дүрслэл), quotation (эшлэл), and notes, matching structure and style to reader and purpose.
- Idioms, imagery, and quotations are rewarded, not penalised.
- Structure: эхлэл / үндсэн хэсэг / дүгнэлт; the conclusion restates and adds nothing new.
- Openers like "Өнөөгийн нийгэмд..." are what many students are taught. Do not cut them unless the user asks for a fresher opening.

**What to fix in essays:** translationese, choppy -сан runs, and lifeless voice (voice.md). If the essay has no personal angle, suggest where one would go; do not invent it.

## The word ladder [эх сурвалж]

| Эрхэмсэг (elevated) | Ерийн (neutral) | Доромж (derogatory) |
|---|---|---|
| зооглох | идэх | гудрах |
| айлдах | хэлэх | чалчих |
| морилох | явах | сажлах |

- Most modern prose uses the neutral rung.
- Honorific words (айлтгах, бараалхах, морилох, өргөх) suit addressing elders, superiors, or ceremony. Do not sprinkle them into an ordinary essay.
- Never move a word down a rung in a rewrite.

## Tone guard: check every rewrite

A rewrite fails if any answer is "yes":

1. Did I label a group of people ("боловсролгүй хүн", "хойрго ажилчид") where the original did not?
2. Did a framed or softened statement become a flat verdict?
3. Did I drop "Та", a courtesy formula, "гэж үзэж байна", "болов уу", "-даг юм", or "шүү дээ" that the original had?
4. Did I use a lower-rung or more colloquial word than the original?
5. Did a balanced sentence become one curt, lopsided clause?
6. Would a Mongolian teacher reading it aloud find it colder or ruder than the original?

### Worked failure

> **Original:** Өнөөгийн хурдацтай хөгжиж буй технологийн эрин үед боловсрол нь хүний амьдралд маш чухал үүрэг гүйцэтгэдэг юм.
> **Failed rewrite:** Боловсролгүй хүнд өнөөдөр амьдрал хэцүү.

Fails checks 1, 2, 3, and 5. The original was acceptable formal Mongolian and needed no change.
